Privacy Policy

≈ Miscalculator

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Miscalculator does not collect, sell, or store any personal information itself. The one thing it does that touches a third party is showing ads, described below.

What the app stores

  • Your current calculation lives only in the app's memory while you're using it. Nothing about what you calculate is saved, logged, or sent anywhere.
  • No account, no sign-in, no personal information is ever requested.

Advertising

Miscalculator shows optional rewarded video ads (the "watch an ad for the real answer" feature) using Google's AdMob SDK. To serve and measure these ads, Google's SDK may collect device and advertising identifiers, approximate location, and app usage/diagnostic data on its own behalf — this happens whether or not you choose to watch an ad, since the SDK loads ads in the background.

Miscalculator does not request permission to track you across other apps or websites (no App Tracking Transparency prompt), so ads are served without access to your device's advertising identifier for cross-app tracking. Google may still process limited technical data to serve non-personalized, contextual ads and to prevent fraud. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services for details.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics SDKs
  • No account creation
  • No collection of your calculations, contacts, photos, or location by us
  • No selling of any data — we don't have any to sell

Children's privacy

Miscalculator is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. Because it includes third-party advertising, it is not intended for use by children under 13 without a parent or guardian's involvement.
