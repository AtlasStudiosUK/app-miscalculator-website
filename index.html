Miscalculator

≈ Support

Miscalculator looks exactly like a normal calculator. It isn't one — every answer is deliberately scrambled to land somewhere within about 20% of the real result. It's a joke app, and it's supposed to do that.

Frequently asked

Is my calculator broken?

No — Miscalculator is designed to always be wrong, by up to 20% in either direction. That's the entire app. If you need a calculator that gets the right answer, use the one that came with your phone.

How do I see the real answer?

Tap "Watch ad for real answer" underneath a result. After the ad, Miscalculator will show you the true value for that one calculation — in green, so you know it's the honest one.

Why does the equals button say "≈" instead of "="?

Fair warning. "≈" means "approximately equal to," which is the most honest symbol this app could put on that button.

Does it ever show the correct answer by accident?

No — Miscalculator specifically avoids landing on the exact right answer, even by chance.
